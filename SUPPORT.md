# Support

Mind Seed Systems does not currently offer a public service-level agreement,
warranty, paid support, or general compatibility guarantee.

For a non-sensitive problem with a public repository, open an issue in that
repository and include a minimal reproduction, the affected version or commit,
and redacted evidence.

For suspected vulnerabilities or any report containing sensitive information,
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not open a public issue.
